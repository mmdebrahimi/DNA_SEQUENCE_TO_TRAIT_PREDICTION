**THE SEROVAR O-AXIS GAP IS REFERENCE-DB CONTENT, AND MY OWN THRESHOLD CHANGE IS EXONERATED TWICE (2026-09-09).** The SeqSero2 run localized the caller's entire −0.175 gap to the O axis and recorded it as a **lead, not a diagnosis**. This is the diagnosis. **THE HYPOTHESIS I STARTED WITH WAS MINE AND IT WAS WRONG:** I lowered this caller's O coverage cut 80→40 on 2026-09-04, so partial alignments were the obvious suspect — **tested twice, falsified twice.** The `9,46` winner is `O__9,46__239` at **99.70 id / 100.00 cov** (full-length, near-perfect), and `O__1,3,19__126` hits **121/200 at EVERY cut from 40 through 90** because where it hits its coverage is 99.2–100%. **TWO STRUCTURAL DB DEFECTS INSTEAD.** (1) **There is no plain O9 allele** — `grep -c ">O__9__"` = **0**; the DB has `9,46` and `9,46,27` but not `9`, while the reference tool calls O=9 on **17/187** cohort genomes. O group 9 (D1: Enteritidis, Typhi, Dublin) is among the most prevalent in Salmonella, so those genomes are forced onto the nearest allele — exactly the `9,46→9` pattern, 15 of 33 O disagreements. **No threshold or selection change can fix a missing reference sequence.** (2) **`O__1,3,19__126` is 130 bp against an O-allele median of 1155** — the shortest in the DB — aligning at 99.2–100% coverage on **60%** of a diverse cohort where its antigen's true prevalence is **2%**, i.e. **28× over**; our caller emits `1,3,19` on 14 genomes where the reference says 4. **THE CHECK WAS WRONG BEFORE THE ALLELE WAS:** the first audit used a flat ">25% of cohort" bar and also flagged `O__4__231` — but O=4 genuinely IS 32% of this cohort, so a 32% hit-rate is CORRECT. A flat bar cannot separate promiscuity from prevalence; the audit now measures hit-rate **against each antigen's own reference prevalence** (`O__4__231` clears at 1×). **The false flag was removed before publishing, not after.** **ROOT CAUSE CORRECTED 2026-09-09 — THE TWO 'DEFECTS' ARE ONE, AND MY 'EXTERNAL WALL' WAS WRONG (`wiki/salmserovar_db_semantics_2026-09-09.md`):** our antigen DB **IS SeqSero2's, re-headered** — **all 62 O alleles are BYTE-IDENTICAL to a SeqSero2 entry**. SeqSero2 names entries with a SEMANTIC QUALIFIER; our schema `O__<antigen>__<index>` has no field for one, so the conversion **discarded it**. Both flagged alleles are qualified entries, confirmed byte-identical: `O__1,3,19__126` **is** `O-1,3,19_not_in_3,10__130` (a DIFFERENTIAL marker — present in 1,3,19, absent in 3,10) and `O__9,46__362` **is** `O-9,46_wzy_partial__216`. So the caller asserts a differential marker as a positive `1,3,19` call (the 60% / 28×-over-prevalence hit-rate), and the `wbaV` entries — which is how SeqSero2 distinguishes O9 from O9,46 — flatten into ordinary `9,46` alleles so **plain O9 becomes unemittable**. **SeqSero2 has NO plain O9 allele either**, so 'add a missing O9 sequence' was the wrong fix to reach for. **THIS IS CODE/DATA-BUILD CLOSABLE, NOT EXTERNAL** — every sequence is already present and byte-correct; what is missing is preserving the qualifier and honoring it (differential O typing). **THIRD TIME THIS SESSION I CALLED A WALL TOO EARLY** (after ktype's 'cohort blocker' and my own coverage threshold) — the pattern is reaching for EXTERNAL before exhausting READABLE. **STEP 1 (READ THE SOURCE) IS NOW DONE AND IT CORRECTED TWO CLAIMS — INCLUDING ONE OF MINE (`wiki/seqsero2_o_algorithm_reading_2026-09-09.md`, 2026-09-09).** Read from the pinned container (`call_O_and_H_type` L1070-1131 + `get_kmer_dict` L1053-1069). **(a) THE THRESHOLDS ARE TRANSLATABLE:** SeqSero2's score is `len(ref_kmers & input_kmers)/len(ref_kmers)*100` — a **coverage-of-reference** quantity, the same axis as our `cov = alignment_length/qlen*100`. So membership `>25`, `wbaV >70`, `wzy >40` map onto BLAST **coverage** (our cut of 40 is STRICTER than SeqSero2's admission bar of 25). Argued equivalence on one axis, **not measured** — validate before trusting. **(b) THE PLAN IS NOT UNIMPLEMENTABLE:** `tyr` only splits **O9 from O2**, both INSIDE the wbaV branch; the determinant reaching `O-9` is **`wbaV`**, and plain `O-9` is that branch's **DEFAULT**.
